Netlify:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
3. Drag the extracted folder onto Netlify Drop.
4. After publishing, open Domain settings and add bullhunterinsight.com.
5. At your domain registrar, add the DNS records Netlify shows.
6. Enable HTTPS in Netlify.

The site is static and requires no build command.
